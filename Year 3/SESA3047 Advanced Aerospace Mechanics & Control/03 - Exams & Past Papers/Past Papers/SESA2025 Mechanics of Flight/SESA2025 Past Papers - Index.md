# SESA2025 Past Papers - Index

SESA2025 Mechanics of Flight was the second-year flight mechanics module that SESA2027 replaced. Much of the SESA3047 content from Chapter 5 onwards (Dr Sean Symon's six weeks from Mon 2 Nov 2026: state-space models, eigenvalues and the longitudinal and lateral modes) also appears in these papers, so they are the recommended practice papers for that half of the module (L14, ll. 19–23). SESA2025 assessed slightly different things, though: each paper also has static-stability, trim-drag and turn-performance questions, which are SESA2027 revision ([[SESA2027 Aerospace Mechanics & Control Hub]]) rather than SESA3047 material.

Most papers are two hours, four questions of 25 marks, answer all, with a formula sheet at the end. The exceptions: 2016-17 and 2017-18 have three questions (40, 30 and 30 marks); 2013-14 has no formula sheet, so its solutions use the 2014-15 sheet; and 2020-21 was a 24-hour online open-book test (30, 20, 25 and 25 marks) in which each student downloaded their own data set, so its Q1(ii), Q3 and Q4 are worked with stand-in data (the F-4C and Navion of the Chapter 6 notes), flagged wherever it is used.

## When each topic becomes doable

State-space and eigenvalue parts need [[SESA3047 5.4 - Time-Domain Solution and Stability]]. Phugoid and short-period parts need [[SESA3047 6.1 - Longitudinal Modes, Eigenvectors and the SPO Approximation]], and the phugoid approximation (§6.6) is due on Mon 16 Nov. Dutch roll, spiral and roll-subsidence parts wait for the lateral modes (§§6.7–6.10), due on Thu 19 Nov. The 2020-21 to 2023-24 papers are the closest match, since they are the ones with explicit state-space questions.
