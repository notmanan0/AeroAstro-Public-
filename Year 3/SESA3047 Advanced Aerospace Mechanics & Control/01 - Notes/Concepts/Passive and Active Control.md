# Passive and Active Control

**Passive control** relies on a system's inherent mechanical properties or its natural interaction with the environment. It needs no continuous measurement, real-time decisions or powered actuation. Damping treatments, tuned mass absorbers, an aircraft's inherent stability, spin stabilisation and passive nutation dampers are all passive.

**Active control** uses a power source, sensors, a controller and actuators to generate commanded forces or moments: flight-control surfaces, reaction wheels, magnetic torquers, active vibration actuators.

Real vehicles usually combine the two. The passive design sets the uncontrolled dynamics, and active control then modifies them to meet tighter requirements.
