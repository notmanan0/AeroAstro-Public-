# Passive and Active Control

## Passive control

Uses inherent mechanical properties or natural environmental interaction. It needs no continuous measurement, real-time decision or powered commanded actuation.

Examples: damping treatment, tuned mass absorber, inherent aircraft stability, spin stabilisation, passive nutation damping.

## Active control

Uses an external power source, sensors, a controller and actuators to generate commanded forces or moments.

Examples: flight-control surfaces, reaction wheels, magnetic torquers and active vibration actuators.

## Relationship

Real vehicles often combine both. Passive design sets the uncontrolled dynamics; active control modifies the result to meet tighter requirements.
