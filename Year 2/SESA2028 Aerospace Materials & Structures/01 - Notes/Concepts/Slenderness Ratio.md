# Slenderness Ratio

The radius of gyration and slenderness are

$$
r_g=\sqrt{I/A},\qquad \lambda=\frac{KL}{r_g}.
$$

Euler stress can then be written

$$
\sigma_E=\frac{\pi^2E}{\lambda^2}.
$$

Large $\lambda$ means elastic buckling is likely to govern; small $\lambda$ shifts attention toward yielding, crushing or inelastic buckling. For asymmetric sections calculate slenderness about the relevant principal directions and use the largest value.

See [[Euler Buckling]].
