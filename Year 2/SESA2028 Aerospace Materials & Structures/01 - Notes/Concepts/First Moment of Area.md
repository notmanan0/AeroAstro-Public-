# First Moment of Area

The first moment of an accumulated area is

$$
Q_y=\int z\,dA,\qquad Q_z=\int y\,dA.
$$

In thin walls $dA=t\,ds$. The important word is *accumulated*: begin at a free edge and integrate along the actual wall path to the cut. In the familiar symmetric formula $q=VQ/I$, $Q$ uses the coordinate perpendicular to the neutral axis associated with the shear force.

At the end of a complete open contour, the centroidal first moments return to zero, so $q$ returns to zero at the second free edge.

See [[Shear Flow]].
