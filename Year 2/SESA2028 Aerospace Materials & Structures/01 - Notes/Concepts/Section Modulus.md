# Section Modulus

For bending about a principal symmetry axis,

$$
Z=\frac{I}{c},\qquad \sigma_{max}=\frac{M}{Z}.
$$

Use separate positive- and negative-side moduli when the extreme distances differ. The scalar shortcut is not generally valid in unsymmetrical bending, where stress depends on both coordinates and $I_{yz}$; use the full coupled field instead.

Section modulus measures strength efficiency, whereas $EI$ measures bending stiffness. A section can be strong enough but too flexible.
