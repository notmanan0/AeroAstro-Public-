# Saint-Venant Torsion

Saint-Venant torsion describes the part of torsional response associated with free warping away from end disturbances:

$$
\frac{d\phi}{dx}=\frac{T}{GJ}.
$$

For circular shafts $J$ is the polar second moment and $\tau=Tr/J$. For non-circular sections $J$ is a torsion constant, not generally the polar second moment. Thin open and thin closed sections require different approximations.

Near clamps, load introduction points or diaphragms, restrained warping may add longitudinal normal stress beyond the Saint-Venant solution.

See [[Open-Section Torsion]] and [[Bredt-Batho Torsion]].
