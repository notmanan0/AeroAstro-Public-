# Polar Strain-Displacement Relations

For axisymmetric radial displacement $u(r)$,

$$
\varepsilon_r=\frac{du}{dr},\qquad
\varepsilon_\theta=\frac ur,
$$

with zero $r\theta$ shear strain. The hoop strain exists because a radial displacement changes the circumference.

Regularity at the centre of a solid disc requires finite $u$, which removes any (1/r) displacement term. In a hollow cylinder such a term is admissible because $r=0$ is outside the domain.

See [[SESA2028 S9 - Continuum Mechanics in Cylindrical Coordinates]].
