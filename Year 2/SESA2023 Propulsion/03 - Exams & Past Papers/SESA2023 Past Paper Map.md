# SESA2023 Past Paper Map

Where each topic appears across the 11 papers in the vault. There is no 2019-20 paper. Each year links to its full worked solutions.

> [!tip] How to use this
> - **The syllabus changed in 2020-21.**
>   - **2013-19** (older lecturer): Q1 compulsory plus two of three. Heavy on **turbojet/ramjet cycle numbers** and **descriptive essays**: power cycles, SR-71, SABRE, Concorde, materials, ion engines.
>   - **2020-25** (current notes): short-answer theory plus calculation across **gas dynamics, combustion, cycles, turbomachinery and rockets**.
> - **2022-23, 2023-24 and 2024-25 are the best model**: four compulsory 25-mark questions, closed book, with the Thermofluids Data Book (ISA Table 25, gas Table 2, calorific values Table 1).
> - Legacy topics **not** in the current notes: ion propulsion, SABRE, J58, turboprop architecture, APU/ETOPS, rocket power-cycle essays. They are marked "legacy" in [[SESA2023 Propulsion Hub]].

## Recurring question types

> [!example] Bookwork to reproduce cold
> 1. **Ideal ramjet specific thrust** $F/\dot m = M\sqrt{\gamma RT_a}[(1+f)\sqrt{T_{03}/T_{02}}-1]$, via $M_e = M$: 2018-19 (10 marks); used implicitly in all ramjet questions.
> 2. **Efficiency definitions and factorisation** $\eta_O = \eta_P\eta_{th}$, with the meaning of each numerator and denominator: 2014-15, 2015-16, 2017-18 (20 marks), 2018-19.
> 3. **Breguet range**: 2014-15 (12 marks), 2016-17 (10 marks); used in 2023-24 Q3(v).
> 4. **Rocket $I_{sp}$ from the SFEE**: 2013-14, 2014-15. **Tsiolkovsky**: 2024-25.
> 5. **Actuator disk** $V_{disk} = \tfrac12(V+V_j)$: 2023-24.
> 6. **Turbofan specific thrust** $F/\dot m_c = (1+f)V_{jh}+BPR\,V_{jc}-(1+BPR)V$: 2016-17, 2018-19.
> 7. **Equilibrium cubic** from atom balances and $K_p$: 2013-14.

> [!example] Standard calculations (know the recipe)
> - **Turbojet march** (intake → compressor → burner $f$ → turbine work balance → nozzle): 2013-14, 2014-15, 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2018-19 and 2020-21. Plus the turbine-only version in 2024-25 Q3.
> - **Ideal ramjet with a given $f$** (flight Mach number from the burner energy balance): 2013-14, 2014-15, 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18. **With a given $T_{03}$** and $f$ as the unknown: 2021-22 and 2024-25.
> - **Real ramjet with $\Gamma$ values**: 2018-19, 2020-21, and 2023-24 (the reverse: find $\Gamma$ from measurements).
> - **Normal shock**: 2020-21 (argon, $\gamma = 1.67$), 2022-23 (from the pressure ratio). **Choked flow**: 2021-22 (mixture), 2022-23 Q2, 2024-25 Q2(iv).
> - **Velocity triangles and Euler**: 2021-22 (compressor stage), 2022-23 (turbine), 2024-25 (rotor exit static pressure).
> - **Stage count** $n = \Delta h_0/(\psi U^2)$: 2020-21 (LPT), [[SESA2023 Problem Sheet 9 Solutions]] Q9.1.
> - **Similarity scaling**: 2021-22 Q4(ii–iii), Problem Sheet 9 Q9.3–9.4.
> - **Staging** with the $\lambda$–$\delta$ model: 2024-25 Q2, [[SESA2023 Problem Sheet 10 Rockets Solutions]].

> [!warning] Traps seen in these papers
> - **2017-18 Q2 misprint**: "$p_a$ = 7165 kPa" at 44,000 ft. The specific thrust does not depend on $p_a$.
> - **2016-17 Q3(ii)**: the mass labels in Breguet are reversed. Use ln(initial/final).
> - **Fuel-air ratio convention**: the pre-2020 papers imply $T_{ref} = 0$; 2020+ papers state a 298 K reference. Say which you use.
> - **Gas properties**: 2020-21 Q3 (products $c_p = 1200$, $\gamma = 1.35$), 2022-23 Q3–Q4 and 2024-25 Q3 give **different** $c_p$ and $\gamma$ for air and products. Use the right one on each side of the work balance.
> - **2021-22 Q2(vi)**: the molar mass of 162 for C₁₀H₂₁ is inconsistent (it should be 141). Use the given value.
> - **Problem Sheet 10 answers**: (d) and (e) are swapped in the key.
