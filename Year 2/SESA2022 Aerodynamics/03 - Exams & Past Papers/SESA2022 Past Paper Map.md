# SESA2022 Past Paper Map

> [!tip] How to use this
> - The **format changed** three times: 2013-20 closed-book (compressible flow included), 2020-24 open-book online (data files, notebooks, per-ID parameters), and 2024-25 back to a 2-hour closed-book paper with a full formula sheet.
> - **2024-25 is the best model for the current exam.**
> - Every paper has one question on each of **BL, potential flow, TAT and finite wings**.

## Recurring question types

> [!example] Bookwork you should be able to reproduce cold
> 1. $c_l = \pi(2A_0+A_1)$ from $\gamma(\theta)$: 2014-15, 2015-16, 2016-17.
> 2. $C_L = \pi AR\,B_1$ and $C_{D_i} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi AR}(1+\delta)$ from $\Gamma(\theta)$: 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2024-25.
> 3. ELD: constant downwash $w = -\Gamma_0/2b$, $C_L = \pi b\Gamma_0/(2VS)$: 2018-19, 2019-20.
> 4. Flat-plate CV giving $D' = \rho U^2\theta$, then the MIE: 2016-17, 2017-18.
> 5. MIE with an assumed profile giving $\theta/x$ and $C_f$: 2024-25 (and the linear-profile lecture example).
> 6. Lifting cylinder: $V_\theta$ and $p(\theta)$, then K–J by integration: 2016-17.

> [!example] Standard calculations
> - **Parabolic camber** $z = \epsilon x(1-x/c)$: $A_1 = \epsilon$, $\alpha_{L=0} = -\epsilon/2$, $C_{m,c/4} = -\pi\epsilon/4$. Appears in **seven** papers: 2014-15, 2015-16, 2016-17, 2017-18, 2019-20, 2022-23 and 2024-25.
> - **25 % flap**: $C_l = 2\pi\alpha+3.826\delta$, $C_{m,c/4} = -0.6495\delta$. Appears in 2013-14, 2018-19 and 2023-24.
> - **Rectangular wing with ELD via twist**: $C_l(0) = \frac4\pi C_L$, $\alpha_{root} = \frac{C_l(0)}{2\pi}+\alpha_{L=0}+\frac{C_L}{\pi AR}$. Appears in 2018-19, 2019-20, 2020-21, 2022-23 and 2023-24.
> - **Virtual-origin drag**: 2018-19, 2019-20, 2020-21, 2022-23 and 2024-25.
> - **Superposition, stagnation points, dividing streamline, then $C_p$ from Bernoulli**: every year.
> - **Method of images**: 2013-14 (implicitly), 2020-21, 2021-22 and 2022-23.
