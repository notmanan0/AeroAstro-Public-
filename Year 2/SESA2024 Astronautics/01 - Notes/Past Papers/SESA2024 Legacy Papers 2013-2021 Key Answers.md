> [!warning] Corrections to the earlier version of this note
> - **2019/20 B2(c)**: the far-side array is in **eclipse** ($a\sin7^\circ$ = 878 km < $R_E$), so −68.5 °C, not 27 °C.
> - **2016/17 Q2(iv)–(v)**: the burnout speed of 8.99 km/s is super-circular, so the parking orbit is elliptical ($e$ = 0.345). The apogee raise is 0.756 km/s, not 2.00 km/s (the circular reading is kept as an alternative).
> - **2016/17 Q3**: at midnight the cube is in eclipse, 18.6 °C, not 36 °C.
> - **2017/18 Q4**: (501, 31) is valid (31 is prime), so FOV 19.7° at 230 km. The earlier (502, 31) line was spurious.
> - **2015/16 Q4**: $k$ = 63.98 is rounded **down** to 63, to stay inside the tolerance (consistent with 2021/22 B3).
