# Magnetic Flux and Flux Density

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
>
> $$\Phi = \int\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf A\ \ [\mathrm{Wb} = \mathrm{T\,m^2} = \mathrm{V\,s}],\qquad \Phi = BA\cos\theta\ \text{(uniform)},\qquad \lambda = N\Phi\ \text{(flux linkage)}$$

## Explanation
- $B$ (tesla) is the density of field lines. Flux counts the lines passing **normally** through an area.
- Field lines form closed loops (no monopoles): N → S outside a magnet, S → N inside.
- ⊗ means into the page and ⊙ out of it.
- Machine steel is kept below ~1.5 T to avoid **saturation** ([[Transformer EMF Equation]]).

## Examples
- A 0.1 m² coil in 2 T at angle θ: $\Phi = 0.2\cos\theta$ Wb.
- Magnet passing a coil: the flux is +1, 0, −1, −2 field-line counts as it moves (lecture 2a).
