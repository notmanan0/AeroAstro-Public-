# Argand Diagram

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> The plane in which $z=x+\mathrm jy$ is plotted as the point, or vector, $(x,y)$. The real axis is horizontal and the imaginary axis vertical.

## Explanation
- $|z|$ is the distance from O, and $\arg z$ is the angle from the positive real axis.
- **Addition**: the parallelogram law. $z_1-z_2$ is the vector from $z_2$ to $z_1$, so $|z_1-z_2|$ is the distance between the points.
- $z^*$ is the reflection in the real axis. Multiplying by $\mathrm j$ rotates by $+90°$; multiplying by $e^{\mathrm j\phi}$ rotates by $\phi$.
- The $n$th roots of a number form a regular $n$-gon centred at O.

## Examples
- A square on $\vec{\mathrm{OP}}=1+2\mathrm j$: the other vertices are $\mathrm j(1+2\mathrm j)=-2+\mathrm j$ and $-1+3\mathrm j$ (Ex 4.15).
- The sum and difference construction is in Specimen Test 5, Q2.
