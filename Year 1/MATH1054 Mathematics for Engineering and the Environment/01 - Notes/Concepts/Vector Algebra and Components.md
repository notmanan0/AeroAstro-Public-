# Vector Algebra and Components

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $\mathbf a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)=a_1\mathbf i+a_2\mathbf j+a_3\mathbf k$, with
> $$|\mathbf a|=\sqrt{a_1^2+a_2^2+a_3^2},\qquad\hat{\mathbf a}=\frac{\mathbf a}{|\mathbf a|}$$
> The direction cosines $(l,m,n)$ are the components of $\hat{\mathbf a}$.

## Explanation
- Vectors are added componentwise; geometrically, by the triangle or parallelogram law.
- $\vec{\mathrm{AB}}=\mathbf b-\mathbf a$, and a closed loop of vectors sums to $\mathbf0$.
- **Collinear points**: $\vec{\mathrm{PQ}}=\lambda\vec{\mathrm{QR}}$.
- **Resultant and equilibrant** of forces: add the vectors; the equilibrant is the negative of the resultant.
- **Relative velocity**: the velocity of B seen by A is $\mathbf v_B-\mathbf v_A$.

## Examples
- $\mathrm P(2,-1,3)$: $|\mathrm{OP}|=\sqrt{14}$, direction cosines $\frac1{\sqrt{14}}(2,-1,3)$ (Ex 4.1).
- A NW wind of 50 kn and airspeed 400 kn: head $5.07°$ N of W; ground speed 363 kn (Ex 4.7).
