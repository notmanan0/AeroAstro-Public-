# Scalar (Dot) Product

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $$\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|\cos\theta=a_1b_1+a_2b_2+a_3b_3$$

## Explanation
- **Angle**: $\cos\theta=\frac{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{|\mathbf a||\mathbf b|}$.
- **Perpendicular** iff $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=0$.
- **Component of $\mathbf F$ along $\mathbf u$**: $\mathbf F\cdot\hat{\mathbf u}$.
- **Work**: $W=\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf d$.
- $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf a=|\mathbf a|^2$. It is commutative and distributive, but you cannot cancel $\mathbf a$ from $\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf c$.

## Examples
- The angle between $(1,2,3)$ and $(2,0,4)$ is $33.2°$ (Ex 4.18).
- Work by $(3,-2,5)$ from P to Q is $3$ (Ex 4.22).
- $\lambda=\frac{14}5$ makes $\mathbf u+\lambda\mathbf k\perp\mathbf v-\lambda\mathbf i$ (Ex 28(c)).
