# Static Margin and Tail Volume

Horizontal-tail volume packages tail authority:

$$\boxed{V_H=\frac{S_Tl_T}{S\bar c}}.$$

Static margin measures CG position relative to the neutral point:

$$\boxed{SM=\frac{x_{NP}-x_{CG}}{\bar c}}.$$

For the usual aft-positive chordwise coordinate:

- $SM>0$: stable;
- $SM=0$: neutral;
- $SM<0$: unstable.

A useful linear relation is

$$C_{m_\alpha}\approx-C_{L_\alpha}SM.$$

Larger $V_H$ tends to move the neutral point aft, but tail efficiency, tail lift slope and wing downwash matter. Forward CG increases margin but also increases trim/control demand.

For the full second-year treatment, see [[Neutral Point and Static Margin]].
