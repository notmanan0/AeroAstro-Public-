# Space Mission Architecture

A mission architecture is the arrangement of payload, spacecraft, orbit/trajectory, launch, ground and operations elements that delivers a defined outcome.

$$\text{stakeholder need}\rightarrow\text{measurement}\rightarrow\text{payload}\rightarrow\text{orbit}\rightarrow\text{system}$$

Key trades couple the whole system:

- altitude: coverage/revisit versus resolution, link loss, radiation and launch energy;
- constellation size: revisit/resilience versus cost and operations;
- onboard processing: downlink reduction versus power, heat and software risk;
- propulsion: manoeuvre capability versus mass, power and lifetime;
- redundancy: reliability versus mass, cost and complexity.

A good architecture states success criteria, margins, failure tolerance, verification route and end-of-life plan.
