# Minimum Drag and Minimum Power

For level flight with a parabolic polar,

$$D=AV^2+\frac{B}{V^2},\qquad P_R=DV=AV^3+\frac BV.$$

| Optimum | Drag-component condition | Lift coefficient |
|---|---|---|
| minimum drag | $D_i=D_0$ | $\sqrt{C_{D0}/k}$ |
| minimum power | $D_i=3D_0$ | $\sqrt{3C_{D0}/k}$ |

$$\boxed{V_{MP}=3^{-1/4}V_{MD}\approx0.760V_{MD}}.$$

- Minimum drag equals maximum $L/D$ and best still-air glide angle.
- Minimum power equals minimum sink and ideal propeller endurance.

> [!warning] Feasibility
> An aerodynamic optimum below stall speed or outside the propulsion/control envelope is not flyable.
