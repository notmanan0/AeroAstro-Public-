# Launch Vehicle Interfaces

Payload compatibility is checked across six linked interfaces:

1. **mission:** orbit, epoch, dispersions and separation state;
2. **mechanical:** mass, CG, inertia, adapter and fairing envelope;
3. **loads:** static, coupled, acoustic, vibration and shock;
4. **electrical/data:** power, commands, telemetry, grounding and inhibits;
5. **environmental:** temperature, humidity, pressure, cleanliness and purge;
6. **operations:** transport, integration, access, countdown and contingency.

Interface-control documents baseline responsibilities and allowable values. A change in payload mass properties can alter coupled loads and guidance even when total mass remains within limit.

> [!warning] Payload capacity
> A launcher’s advertised payload is conditional on orbit, inclination, site, fairing, recovery mode and mission reserves.
