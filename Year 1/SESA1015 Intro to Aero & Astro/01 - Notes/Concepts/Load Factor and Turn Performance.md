# Load Factor and Turn Performance

For a coordinated level turn,

$$\boxed{n=\frac LW=\frac1{\cos\phi}}.$$

$$\boxed{R=\frac{V^2}{g\tan\phi}=\frac{V^2}{g\sqrt{n^2-1}}},\qquad
\boxed{\dot\psi=\frac{g\tan\phi}{V}}.$$

The accelerated stall relation is

$$\boxed{V_{s,n}=V_s\sqrt n}.$$

Induced drag grows approximately as $n^2$ at fixed speed, so a lift-limited bank angle may not be sustainable with the available thrust.

### Example

At $60^\circ$ bank, $n=2$. The pilot/structure feels a 2-g load, and stall speed is 41.4% higher than the 1-g value.

The achievable turn is bounded by $C_{L,max}$, structural $n_{limit}$ and propulsion.
