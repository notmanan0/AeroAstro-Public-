# Space Environment Hazards

| Environment | Principal effect | Response |
|---|---|---|
| solar/planetary radiation | heating, thermal cycles | optical properties, radiators, heaters |
| ionising particles | TID, displacement, SEE | shielding, rad-tolerant parts, EDAC |
| vacuum | outgassing, lubricant loss | material screening, bake-out |
| plasma | charging and arcing | bonding, conductive surfaces |
| atomic oxygen | surface erosion | protective coatings |
| debris/MMOD | hypervelocity impact | shielding, avoidance, redundancy |

Risk depends on orbit, duration, attitude, solar cycle and shielding. The correct chain is

$$\text{environment}\rightarrow\text{component load/dose}\rightarrow\text{failure mode}\rightarrow\text{mitigation and verification}.$$

> [!important] Extremes and accumulation
> Some requirements are peak survival cases; others are accumulated dose or cycle counts. An average environment is not sufficient for both.
