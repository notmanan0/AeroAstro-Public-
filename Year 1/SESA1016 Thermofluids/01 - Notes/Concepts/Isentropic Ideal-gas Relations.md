# Isentropic Ideal-gas Relations

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> For a reversible adiabatic, calorically perfect ideal gas: $pv^\gamma=C$, $Tv^{\gamma-1}=C$ and $T^\gamma p^{1-\gamma}=C$.

## Application

$$
\frac{T_2}{T_1}=\left(\frac{v_1}{v_2}\right)^{\gamma-1}
=\left(\frac{p_2}{p_1}\right)^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}.
$$

These relations require ideal-gas behaviour, constant specific heats and $s_2=s_1$. They do not apply to an irreversible adiabatic process.
