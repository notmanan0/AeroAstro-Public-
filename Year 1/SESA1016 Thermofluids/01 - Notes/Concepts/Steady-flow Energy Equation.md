# Steady-flow Energy Equation

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> For one inlet and outlet, $q-w_s=(h_2-h_1)+(V_2^2-V_1^2)/2+g(z_2-z_1)$.

## Explanation

Enthalpy includes internal energy and flow work. Cross out terms only after stating physical assumptions. Nozzles mainly convert enthalpy to kinetic energy; turbines convert enthalpy to shaft work; heaters convert heat transfer to enthalpy rise.
