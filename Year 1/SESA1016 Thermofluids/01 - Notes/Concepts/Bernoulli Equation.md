# Bernoulli Equation

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> For steady, incompressible, inviscid flow with no mechanical loss or shaft work, $p+\tfrac12\rho V^2+\rho gz$ is constant along a streamline.

## Explanation

It expresses conversion among pressure, kinetic and gravitational potential energy per unit volume. It is not valid through a viscous wake, long lossy pipe, pump or turbine unless the appropriate terms are added.
