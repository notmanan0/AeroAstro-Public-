# Ideal-gas Law

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $pV=mRT$, or $p=\rho RT$, is an equation of state for a dilute gas with negligible intermolecular volume and forces.

## Explanation

- Use absolute pressure and kelvin.
- $R$ is species-specific; for air $R\approx287\ \mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}}$.
- For fixed mass, $p_1V_1/T_1=p_2V_2/T_2$.
- It is a state relation, not a process law.

## Example

At fixed volume, $p\propto T$; at fixed temperature, $pV=$ constant.
