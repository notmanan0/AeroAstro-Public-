# Newtonian Fluid and Viscosity

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> A Newtonian fluid has shear stress proportional to shear rate: $\tau=\mu,du/dy$. Kinematic viscosity is $\nu=\mu/\rho$.

## Explanation

$\mu$ has units Pa s; $\nu$ has units $\mathrm{m^2/s}$. Liquid viscosity generally falls with temperature, while gas viscosity generally rises. The no-slip condition creates velocity gradients and wall shear.
