# Streamline, Pathline and Streakline

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> A streamline is instantaneously tangent to velocity; a pathline is one particle's trajectory; a streakline joins particles that previously passed through a fixed point.

## Explanation

In steady flow all three coincide. In unsteady flow they generally differ. In 2D, streamline slope satisfies $dy/dx=v/u$ at the chosen instant.
