# Pressure and Head

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Pressure is normal force per area. Pressure head $p/(\rho g)$ is the height of a column of the same fluid carrying equivalent pressure energy per unit weight.

## Explanation

For a static constant-density fluid, $p+\rho gz=$ constant. Moving downward increases pressure by $\rho gh$. Head units are metres and allow pressure, kinetic energy, elevation, pumps and losses to appear in one equation.
