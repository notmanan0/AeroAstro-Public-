# Closed System vs Control Volume

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> A **closed system** contains a fixed mass, though its boundary may move. A **control volume** is a chosen region in space through whose boundary mass may flow.

## Explanation

- Closed-system energy crosses as heat or work: $\Delta U=Q-W$.
- A control volume also exchanges energy and momentum through mass flux.
- Piston-cylinder processes are naturally closed systems; nozzles, turbines, pipes and jets are naturally control volumes.
- The boundary must be drawn before selecting equations.

## Example

The gas trapped below a sealed piston is a closed system. The same cylinder treated from inlet valve to exhaust valve during continuous operation is a control volume.
