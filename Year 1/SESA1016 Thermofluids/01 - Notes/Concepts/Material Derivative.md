# Material Derivative

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $D\phi/Dt=\partial\phi/\partial t+\mathbf V\cdot\nabla\phi$ is the rate of change experienced by a moving fluid particle.

## Explanation

The first term is local change at a fixed point. The second is convective change caused by motion through a spatial gradient. Fluid acceleration is $D\mathbf V/Dt$; it can be nonzero in a steady but nonuniform flow.
