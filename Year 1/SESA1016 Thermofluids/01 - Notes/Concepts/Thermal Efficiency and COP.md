# Thermal Efficiency and COP

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> A heat engine has $\eta=W_{net,out}/Q_H=1-Q_C/Q_H$. A refrigerator has $COP_R=Q_C/W_{net,in}$; a heat pump has $COP_{HP}=Q_H/W_{net,in}$.

## Explanation

Efficiency is bounded below one. COP can exceed one because it measures heat transferred, not work conversion. For any refrigerator/heat pump, $COP_{HP}=COP_R+1$.

Carnot limits between $T_H$ and $T_C$ are obtained by replacing heat ratios with absolute-temperature ratios.
