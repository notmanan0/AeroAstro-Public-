# Mass Flow Rate

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $\dot m=\int_A\rho\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n\,dA$ is the mass crossing a surface per unit time.

## Explanation

For uniform properties, $\dot m=\rho\bar VA$. Volume flow $\dot V=\bar VA$ is conserved only for incompressible flow; mass flow is always conserved.

For a circular nonuniform profile, use $dA=2\pi r\,dr$.
