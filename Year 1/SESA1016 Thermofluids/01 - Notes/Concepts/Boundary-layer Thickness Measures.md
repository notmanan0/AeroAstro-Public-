# Boundary-layer Thickness Measures

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $\delta^*=\int(1-u/U_e)dy$ measures mass-flow deficit; $\theta=\int(u/U_e)(1-u/U_e)dy$ measures momentum deficit; $H=\delta^*/\theta$ measures profile shape.

## Explanation

The conventional thickness $\delta$ marks where $u\approx0.99U_e$. Integral thicknesses avoid an arbitrary edge and enter conservation relations directly. A fuller turbulent profile normally has smaller $H$ than a laminar profile.
