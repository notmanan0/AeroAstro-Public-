# Control-volume Analysis Workflow

## Method

1. Draw a boundary that cuts ports where properties are known or plausibly uniform.
2. Choose axes and outward normals.
3. State steady/unsteady, one-dimensional, incompressible and adiabatic assumptions.
4. Apply mass conservation first.
5. Draw external forces before momentum conservation.
6. Retain heat, shaft work, enthalpy, kinetic and potential terms before simplifying energy.
7. Reverse the sign if the requested force is on the hardware rather than on the fluid.
