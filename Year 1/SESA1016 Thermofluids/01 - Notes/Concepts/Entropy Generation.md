# Entropy Generation

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Entropy generation measures irreversibility: $\Delta S=\int\delta Q/T_b+S_{gen}$ with $S_{gen}\ge0$.

## Explanation

Friction, mixing, unrestrained expansion and heat transfer across a finite temperature difference generate entropy. A reversible process has $S_{gen}=0$; a proposed process with $S_{gen}<0$ is impossible.

Adiabatic means zero entropy **transfer with heat**, not necessarily zero entropy change.

## Example

Heat $Q$ moving from $T_H$ to $T_C$ generates $Q(1/T_C-1/T_H)>0$.
