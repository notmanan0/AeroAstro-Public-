# Boundary Work

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> For a quasi-equilibrium moving boundary, $\delta W=p\,dV$ and $W_{1\to2}=\int_{V_1}^{V_2}p\,dV$.

## Explanation

It is the signed area below the process path on a $p$-$V$ diagram. Under the course convention, expansion work is positive and compression work negative. A rigid vessel has zero boundary work even if its pressure changes.

## Examples

- constant pressure: $W=p(V_2-V_1)$;
- isothermal ideal gas: $W=mRT\ln(V_2/V_1)$.
