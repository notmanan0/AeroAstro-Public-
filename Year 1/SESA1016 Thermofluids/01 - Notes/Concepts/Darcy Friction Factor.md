# Darcy Friction Factor

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> The Darcy friction factor appears in $h_f=f(L/D)V^2/(2g)$ and measures distributed wall-friction loss.

## Explanation

For fully developed laminar circular-pipe flow, $f=64/Re_D$. For turbulent flow, $f$ depends on $Re_D$ and relative roughness $\varepsilon/D$ through the Moody chart or Colebrook equation.

The Darcy factor is four times the Fanning factor.
