# Reynolds Number

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $Re=\rho VL/\mu=VL/\nu$ compares inertial with viscous effects.

## Explanation

The characteristic $V$ and $L$ must match the problem: mean velocity and diameter for pipe flow; freestream velocity and distance/chord for external flow. Equal Reynolds number is a key dynamic-similarity condition.

Transition thresholds depend on geometry and disturbances; $Re$ predicts regime only in context.
