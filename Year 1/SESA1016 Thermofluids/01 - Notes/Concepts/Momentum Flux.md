# Momentum Flux

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Momentum crosses a control surface at rate $\int_A\rho\mathbf V(\mathbf V\cdot\mathbf n)dA$; for a uniform port it is $\dot m\mathbf V$.

## Explanation

Momentum is a vector, so direction matters twice: the sign of mass crossing and the direction of the carried velocity. A nonuniform profile requires integration or a momentum correction factor $\beta$.
