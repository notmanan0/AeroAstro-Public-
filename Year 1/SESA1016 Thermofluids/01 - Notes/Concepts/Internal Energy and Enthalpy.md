# Internal Energy and Enthalpy

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Internal energy $U$ is microscopic stored energy. Enthalpy $H=U+pV$ combines internal energy with the pressure-volume energy required to create space for a flowing fluid.

## Explanation

For a calorically perfect ideal gas:

$$
\Delta U=mc_v\Delta T,\qquad \Delta H=mc_p\Delta T.
$$

$U$ is natural for closed rigid systems. $h=u+pv$ is natural for open systems because flowing mass transports both internal energy and flow work.
