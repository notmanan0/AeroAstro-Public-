# Pressure Coefficient

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> $C_p=(p-p_\infty)/(\tfrac12\rho V_\infty^2)$ nondimensionalises local pressure by freestream dynamic pressure.

## Explanation

For incompressible potential flow, $C_p=1-(V/V_\infty)^2$. An ideal stagnation point has $C_p=1$; accelerated regions usually have $C_p<0$. Surface integration of pressure coefficients gives pressure-force coefficients.
