# Major and Minor Head Losses

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Distributed pipe loss is $h_f=f(L/D)V^2/(2g)$; a local fitting loss is $h_m=KV^2/(2g)$.

## Explanation

Total loss is the sum of positive terms. “Minor” means local, not necessarily small. The extended Bernoulli equation includes pump head added, turbine head removed and total loss removed.
