# Skin-friction and Pressure Drag

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Skin-friction drag is the streamwise component of wall shear; pressure drag is the streamwise component of surface pressure forces.

## Explanation

Separation creates a low-pressure wake and large pressure drag. Turbulence increases wall shear but can delay separation, sometimes reducing total drag. Total surface force is $\int_S(-p\mathbf n+\boldsymbol\tau_w)dS$.
