# Stagnation Pressure and Pitot Tube

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> Stagnation pressure is the pressure obtained by decelerating a flow isentropically to zero speed. For incompressible flow at equal elevation, $p_0=p+\tfrac12\rho V^2$.

## Application

A Pitot-static probe measures $p_0-p$, giving

$$
V=\sqrt{\frac{2(p_0-p)}\rho}.
$$

The formula needs an incompressible, low-loss approach to the probe; compressibility corrections are needed at higher Mach number.
