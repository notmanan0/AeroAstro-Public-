# Dislocations and Slip Systems

## Definition
> [!note] Definition
> A dislocation is a line defect characterised by a Burgers vector. Plastic glide moves it on a slip plane/direction, changing neighbours one atomic row at a time.

## Explanation
- Edge: Burgers vector perpendicular to the dislocation line.
- Screw: Burgers vector parallel to the line.
- Slip is easiest on dense planes/directions: FCC $\{111\}\langle110\rangle$ supplies 12 systems.
- BCC/HCP glide is more temperature-sensitive; twinning can supplement slip.
- Yield strength is the stress required to start substantial dislocation motion.
