# Newton's Laws of Motion

## Definition

> [!note] Definition
> 1. A particle with no resultant force stays at rest or moves with constant velocity.
> 2. $\sum\mathbf F = m\mathbf a$, with $\mathbf a$ measured in an **inertial** (non-accelerating) frame.
> 3. The forces two particles exert on each other are equal, opposite and collinear.

## Explanation

- **Procedure**:
  1. Choose positive directions.
  2. Draw the free-body diagram (all external forces).
  3. Draw the kinetic diagram ($m\mathbf a$ in the positive direction).
  4. Equate the components.
- **Mass** is inertia, resistance to changes of velocity, and is absolute. **Weight** $W = mg$ depends on the local field.
- Near the Earth the ground is treated as inertial (its rotation is ignored). For spacecraft the frame is "fixed to the stars".
- In $n$–$t$ form: $\sum F_t = m\dot v$, $\sum F_n = mv^2/\rho$, $\sum F_b = 0$. There is no "centrifugal force" in an inertial frame.
- Integrating over time gives impulse–momentum; integrating over distance gives work–energy.

## Examples

- **Rope tension**: $T = mg$ at rest but $T = m(g + a)$ accelerating upwards.
- **Handcart** (Lecture 8): $P\cos60° = ma_G$ gives $a_G = 0.125$ m/s².
