# Miller Indices for Planes and Directions

## Definition
- Direction $[uvw]$: vector components in lattice units, reduced to smallest integers.
- Plane $(hkl)$: reciprocals of axis intercepts, reduced to smallest integers.
- $\langle uvw\rangle$ and $\{hkl\}$ denote symmetry-equivalent families; a bar marks a negative index.

## Explanation
- Infinite plane intercept $\Rightarrow$ index 0.
- In cubic crystals only, $[hkl]$ is normal to $(hkl)$.
- Slip systems combine a plane and direction, e.g. FCC $\{111\}\langle110\rangle$.
