# Crystal Structures and Atomic Packing Factor

## Definition
> [!note] Definition
> $$APF=\frac{N(4\pi R^3/3)}{V_{cell}}$$
> FCC: $N=4$, $a=2\sqrt2R$, APF 0.740. BCC: $N=2$, $a=4R/\sqrt3$, APF 0.680. HCP is close packed with APF 0.740.

## Explanation
- FCC is ABCABC stacked; HCP is ABAB.
- FCC has many close-packed slip systems; BCC has no close-packed plane; HCP has limited independent easy slip.
- Polymorphism means one element changes crystal structure with temperature, as iron does between BCC and FCC.
