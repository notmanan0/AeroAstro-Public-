# Ductile-to-Brittle Transition

## Definition
At low temperature in BCC/HCP metals, thermally activated slip becomes difficult and $\sigma_y$ rises above the cleavage/fracture stress. Fracture then occurs before appreciable plasticity.

## Explanation
- FCC metals retain many easy slip systems and normally show no sharp DBTT.
- Work, solution and precipitation hardening raise $\sigma_y$ and tend to raise DBTT.
- Grain refinement strengthens while also obstructing cleavage, tending to lower DBTT.
- Charpy absorbed energy maps the transition but is not $K_{IC}$.
