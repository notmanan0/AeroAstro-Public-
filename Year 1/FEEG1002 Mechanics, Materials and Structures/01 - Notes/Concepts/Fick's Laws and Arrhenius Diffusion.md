# Fick's Laws and Arrhenius Diffusion

## Definition
> [!note] Definition
> $$J=-D\frac{dC}{dx},\qquad \frac{\partial C}{\partial t}=D\frac{\partial^2C}{\partial x^2},\qquad D=D_0e^{-Q/RT}$$

## Explanation
- First law: steady flux down a concentration gradient.
- Second law: concentration changes with time; diffusion distance scales as $\sqrt{Dt}$.
- Interstitial diffusion is normally faster than vacancy/substitutional diffusion.
- Grain boundaries are rapid paths because they are disordered and vacancy-rich.
- Use kelvin. $\ln D$ versus $1/T$ has slope $-Q/R$.
