# S-N Curves and Paris Law

## Definition
S-N: stress amplitude versus cycles to failure. Fatigue life is $N$ at specified $S$; fatigue strength is $S$ at specified $N$; some alloys show a fatigue limit.

For a known crack:

$$\Delta K=Y\Delta\sigma\sqrt{\pi a},\qquad \frac{da}{dN}=A(\Delta K)^m$$

## Explanation
Life comprises initiation, propagation and rapid final fracture. Integrating Paris law from detected $a_i$ to critical $a_c$ estimates propagation life. Real S-N data scatter with surface finish, notches, residual stress, mean stress and environment.
