# Iron-Carbon Phase Diagram

## Definition
- $\gamma$: FCC austenite; high-temperature, higher C solubility.
- $\alpha$: BCC ferrite; low C solubility.
- $Fe_3C$: cementite at 6.70 wt% C.
- Eutectoid near 0.76 wt% C, 727°C: $\gamma\to\alpha+Fe_3C$; lamellar product is pearlite.

## Explanation
- Hypoeutectoid: proeutectoid ferrite + pearlite.
- Hypereutectoid: proeutectoid cementite + pearlite.
- Pearlite is a two-phase microconstituent, not a phase.
