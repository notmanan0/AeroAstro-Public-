# Engineering Stress-Strain Properties

## Definition

$$\sigma=F/A_0,\quad\varepsilon=\Delta L/L_0,\quad E=d\sigma/d\varepsilon,\quad \nu=-\varepsilon_t/\varepsilon_a$$

- Yield/proof stress: onset of plasticity.
- UTS: maximum engineering stress and onset of diffuse necking.
- Ductility: elongation or area reduction at fracture.
- Resilience: elastic area, $\sigma_y^2/(2E)$.
- Toughness: total area to fracture.

## Explanation
Engineering stress drops after necking because it uses original area; true stress uses instantaneous area. Toughness is energy per volume and is not fracture toughness $K_{IC}$.
