# Polymer Glass Transition and Viscoelasticity

## Definition
Below $T_g$, segmental chain motion is frozen and an amorphous polymer is glassy. Above $T_g$, motion produces rubbery/viscoelastic response; a thermoplastic flows near $T_m$.

$$\sigma(t)=\sigma_0e^{-t/\tau},\qquad E_r(t)=\sigma(t)/\varepsilon_0$$

## Explanation
- Creep: strain increases at constant stress.
- Stress relaxation: stress decreases at constant strain.
- Long time resembles high temperature because both permit rearrangement.
- Crystallites/crosslinks restrain motion and retain modulus; lag between stress and strain gives damping.
