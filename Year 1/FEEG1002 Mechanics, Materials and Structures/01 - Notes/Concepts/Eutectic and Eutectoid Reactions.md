# Eutectic and Eutectoid Reactions

## Definition
- Eutectic: $L\rightarrow\alpha+\beta$ at one $T_E,C_E$.
- Eutectoid: one solid transforms to two solids, e.g. steel $\gamma\rightarrow\alpha+Fe_3C$.

## Explanation
The two products commonly form fine lamellae so solute need move only a short distance. Hypo-alloys form primary left-hand phase plus eutectic/eutectoid constituent; hyper-alloys form primary right-hand phase plus the invariant constituent.
