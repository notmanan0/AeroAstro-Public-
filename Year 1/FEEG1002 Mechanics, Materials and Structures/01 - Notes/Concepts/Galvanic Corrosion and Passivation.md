# Galvanic Corrosion and Passivation

## Definition
At the anode, $M\to M^{n+}+ne^-$; at the cathode, reduction consumes electrons. Electrically connected dissimilar metals in an electrolyte form a galvanic cell.

## Explanation
- The active metal in the relevant environmental galvanic series corrodes.
- A small anode coupled to a large cathode is severe because current density is high.
- Al, Cr and Ti can appear noble in service despite active standard potentials because adherent oxides passivate them.
- Protection: isolate/coat, avoid crevices, drain, sacrificial anodes, or impressed-current cathodic protection.
