# Precipitation Hardening

## Definition
1. Solution treat in a single-phase field.
2. Quench to retain supersaturated solid solution.
3. Age in the two-phase field to form fine precipitates.

## Explanation
Small coherent particles may be sheared; larger particles force bowing. Peak strength occurs at an optimum size, spacing and coherency. Over-ageing coarsens particles and increases spacing, lowering strength. Kinetics are Arrhenius: hotter ageing peaks faster but may peak lower.
