# Griffith Criterion and Fracture Toughness

## Definition
For an ideal brittle central crack,

$$\sigma_f=\sqrt{\frac{2E\gamma}{\pi a}}$$

Engineering LEFM uses

$$K_I=Y\sigma\sqrt{\pi a},\qquad K_I=K_{IC}\ \text{at plane-strain fracture}$$

## Explanation
Crack growth releases elastic energy but creates surface energy. Longer cracks require less applied stress. $Y$ accounts for geometry/loading; the crack-size convention must match $Y$. $K_{IC}$ is valid only when small-scale yielding and thickness/specimen requirements hold.
