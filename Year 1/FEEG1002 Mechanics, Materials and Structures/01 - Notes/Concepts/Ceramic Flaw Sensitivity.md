# Ceramic Flaw Sensitivity

## Definition
Ceramic tensile strength is controlled by the most severe flaw because strong ionic/covalent bonds restrict plastic crack-tip blunting.

## Explanation
- Tensile results scatter with random flaw size/location; larger volumes have a higher probability of a severe flaw.
- Compression closes flaws, so compressive strength is far larger.
- Design uses a lower statistical percentile/Weibull reliability, not the mean strength.
- Strong bonds provide hardness and environmental resistance but not toughness.
