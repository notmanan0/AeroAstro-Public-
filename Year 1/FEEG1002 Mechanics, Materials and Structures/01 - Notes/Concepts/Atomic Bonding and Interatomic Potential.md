# Atomic Bonding and Interatomic Potential

## Definition
> [!note] Definition
> At equilibrium separation $r_0$, attraction and repulsion balance: $F=-dU/dr=0$. The well depth is bond energy; curvature near $r_0$ controls elastic stiffness.

## Explanation
- Ionic bonds transfer electrons; covalent bonds share them locally; metallic bonds share delocalised electrons.
- Van der Waals and hydrogen bonds are secondary but dominate interchain polymer behaviour.
- Deep wells give high melting temperature; steep wells give high $E$.
- Directional covalent/ionic order restricts slip, explaining why ceramics are stiff but brittle. Non-directional metallic bonds tolerate new neighbours during dislocation glide.
