# Tie Lines and Lever Rule

## Definition
In $\alpha+\beta$, the horizontal tie-line endpoints give $C_\alpha,C_\beta$. For overall $C_0$:

$$f_\alpha=\frac{C_\beta-C_0}{C_\beta-C_\alpha},\qquad f_\beta=\frac{C_0-C_\alpha}{C_\beta-C_\alpha}$$

## Explanation
The phase fraction uses the **opposite** tie-line arm. Compositions and amounts are separate readings. Primary microconstituent fraction just above an invariant temperature is not the same as total phase fraction just below it.

## Checks
- $f_\alpha+f_\beta=1$.
- As $C_0\to C_\alpha$, $f_\alpha\to1$.
