# Annealing of Cold-Worked Metals

## Definition
1. Recovery: dislocations rearrange/annihilate; residual stress falls.
2. Recrystallisation: new dislocation-poor grains nucleate and replace the deformed grains.
3. Grain growth: large grains consume small grains to reduce boundary energy.

## Explanation
Annealing is diffusion controlled. More prior cold work raises the nucleation driving force and usually refines the recrystallised grain size. Higher temperature/longer time accelerates growth and can coarsen the result. Strength/hardness fall while ductility returns.
