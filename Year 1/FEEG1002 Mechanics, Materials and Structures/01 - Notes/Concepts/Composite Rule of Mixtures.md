# Composite Rule of Mixtures

## Definition
For continuous aligned fibres, $V_f+V_m=1$.

Longitudinal isostrain:

$$E_L=V_fE_f+V_mE_m$$

Transverse isostress idealisation:

$$\frac1{E_T}=\frac{V_f}{E_f}+\frac{V_m}{E_m}$$

## Explanation
Load parallel to fibres is shared at common strain; phase loads follow $F_i=E_i\varepsilon V_iA$. Transverse loading forces compliant matrix and stiff fibre to act more like elements in series. The large difference makes the lamina anisotropic. Poor interface, voids, fibre waviness and short/random fibres reduce real performance.
