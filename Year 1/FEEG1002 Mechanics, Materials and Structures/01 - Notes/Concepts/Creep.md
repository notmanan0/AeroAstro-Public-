# Creep

## Definition
Time-dependent strain under sustained stress, important at high homologous temperature.

$$\dot\varepsilon_s=K_2\sigma^n e^{-Q_c/RT}$$

## Explanation
- Primary: rate decreases through work hardening.
- Secondary: near-constant rate; often most service life.
- Tertiary: damage/necking accelerates rate to rupture.
- Mechanisms include diffusion, dislocation climb and grain-boundary sliding; fine grains can harm high-temperature creep resistance.
